The spatial distribution of population in 2024, India

Unconstrained estimates of 2024, total number of people per grid-cell. The dataset is available to download in Geotiff format at a resolution of 3 arc (approximately 100m at the equator). The projection is Geographic Coordinate System, WGS84. The units are number of people per pixel. The mapping approach is Random Forest-based dasymetric redistribution.

Region : India
DOI : 10.5258/SOTON/WP00800
